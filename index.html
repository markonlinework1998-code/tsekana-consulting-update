The people guiding Tsekana Consulting.

Phenyo Hlabe

Chief Executive Officer

Phenyo Hlabe is a strategic and results-driven business leader with a background in Public Management and Governance, Monitoring and Evaluation, project coordination, stakeholder relations, and business development. With extensive experience across research, tender administration, client relationship management, and partnership development, she brings a strong understanding of operational coordination, commercial engagement, and strategic stakeholder management.

As CEO of Tsekana Consulting, she is focused on driving innovation through mining technology and digital solutions, leveraging technology to improve operational efficiency, strengthen business processes, and support smarter, data-driven decision-making within the mining and industrial sectors. Her leadership approach is centred on building strategic partnerships, identifying business opportunities, and translating operational challenges into practical, technology-driven solutions.

Kgaselone Daniel Dikobe

Chairperson

Kgaselone Daniel Dikobe studied Analytical Chemistry at the University of the Witwatersrand (Wits University), laying a strong technical foundation for his career in the industrial, medical, mining and corporate sectors.

He began his professional career at Anchor East before moving to Promax Medical, where he further developed his industry experience. He subsequently worked on projects at Sterlings Project, followed by professional experience at South African Breweries (SAB) and Coca-Cola.

Building on his diverse corporate and technical experience, Daniel transitioned into independent mining project development and management. For more than 13 years, he has independently initiated, developed and pursued mining projects, gaining extensive practical experience in the mining industry, project development and business opportunities.

His combination of analytical knowledge, corporate exposure and over 13 years of independent mining project experience provides a valuable foundation for his leadership role as Chairperson of Tsekana Consulting.
